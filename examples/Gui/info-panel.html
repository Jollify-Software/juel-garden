The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma and is by far the most important source of energy for life on Earth. Click any object in the scene to open its info panel. Click the ✖ button, or click anywhere outside, to close it again. Spin Panels track their mesh on screen, even while the camera moves. This one has close-on-outside-click="false", so only its ✖ button closes it.
